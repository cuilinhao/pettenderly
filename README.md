# Pettenderly

[https://pettenderly.com/](https://pettenderly.com/)

Turn pet photos into portraits worth keeping.

Pettenderly is an online AI pet portrait studio. Upload one to five photos of the same pet, choose a style such as watercolor or Royal Nobility, and download the result. Each successful image uses one credit. Buy a credit pack or subscribe for a monthly allowance.

## 简介

Pettenderly 是在线 AI 宠物写真工具。上传 1–5 张同一只宠物的照片，选择水彩、皇家或影棚风格，生成并下载一幅专属画像。

## How it works

Pettenderly turns everyday pet photos into downloadable AI portraits. Start with one to five clear photos of the same pet, with its eyes and coat markings visible. Choose a watercolor look, a royal costume or a sunny window-seat scene. Create a portrait in the studio, then revisit My creations to download your favorites.

Two beta tools offer more ways to use your photos. You & your pet combines separate person and pet reference photos into a portrait together. Photo cleanup lets you describe a change, such as removing a cable from the background. Faces, markings and fine details can vary in generated images.

Each successful image uses one credit; failed generations do not use credits. Buy a one-time Starter Pack or choose a Pro subscription for a monthly allowance. The result is a digital image you can download and keep.
